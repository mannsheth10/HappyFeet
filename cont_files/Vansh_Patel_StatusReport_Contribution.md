## Vansh Patel Status Report Contribution

My part of Milestone 3 was combining our three cleaned datasets and starting the analysis. Once Jacob, Mann and Shuvam finished cleaning the Census, PLACES and Food Environment Atlas data, I wrote [scripts/integrate.py](../scripts/integrate.py) to merge them on the five-digit FIPS code. The script reads FIPS as text so leading zeros are kept, checks that each file has one row per county, adds a source prefix to the Census columns, and calculates a poverty rate from the Census counts. The merged file is [data/processed/merged_county.csv](../data/processed/merged_county.csv), with 3,134 counties. I also had the script list the 11 counties that do not appear in every dataset, with the reason for each, in [data/processed/merge_unmatched.csv](../data/processed/merge_unmatched.csv), and record row counts and missing values in [data/processed/merge_summary.json](../data/processed/merge_summary.json).

I then wrote [scripts/explore.py](../scripts/explore.py), which produces descriptive statistics, correlations and three figures in the [results](../results) folder. The main early finding is that the overall low-access measure barely correlates with obesity or diabetes, while low access without a car and especially income and poverty correlate much more strongly. I also compared the PLACES and Atlas diabetes figures, as our project plan said we would.

For [StatusReport.md](../StatusReport.md), I renamed the file to the required name, added the task overview table, the integration and exploratory analysis sections, the challenges section, and the team contributions section, turned the timeline into a table with planned and revised dates, and added the changes to our plan. I also added pandas and matplotlib to [requirements.txt](../requirements.txt).
